Our Favorite Pokémon Cards

About This Collection

These are the Pokémon cards our family loves most. Each one shows its era, region, rarity, and set, plus who in the family picked it as a favorite — M for Micah, Z for Zachariah, and H for Howie. A card can be a favorite of more than one person!

The 3 cards below are just examples showing how the page works. To add a real card, take a photo, drop it in the images/cards folder, and add an entry for it in cards.js.
